How to play

↖ ↗
Climb a step up-left or up-right
↙ ↘
Drop a step down-left or down-right
↗ ↗
Tap again mid-air to air jump one more step. Use it to skip gaps and dangers
↖
Up, away from the stairs, hops you back two steps down
- - -
Falling is fine. Falling past the red line ends the run
New tiles and creatures appear as you climb. Each one shows up on its own first, so you can learn it.
